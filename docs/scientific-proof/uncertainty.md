# Uncertainty budget

For every number the app shows: its uncertainty (ε), how it is propagated, the largest term, and the test or
source behind it. Where no ε is known the row says "not quantified" and why; no number here is estimated by
eye. This budget (written 15 Nov 2026 by an SI agent, task P6) and every row in it: **stated as validated by the science lead
on 15 Nov 2026 (no per-check sign-off; see [record](../event/validation.md#validation-status-15-nov))**, with no per-check record of
what was compared. The column "Validation status" below gives that status and, after "before it", the record as it was
before that statement.

Conventions: ε is 1σ unless the row says otherwise. "Per edge" means per start or end of a window (sunlit,
Earth visible, covered). A time error per edge becomes a percentage error of δt / T for a period of length T;
for the default 14-day period, T = 1,209,600 s, so 10 s per edge is 0.0008 percentage points, far below the
0.1 % the app prints.

## Gaps, stated plainly

- **Terrain.** The largest term for light and contact is almost certainly the terrain model (vertical error of
  the LOLA products, the 160 m averages beyond 20 km, the horizon taken at the disc centre's azimuth). It is not
  propagated anywhere in the app. Closing it needs a published vertical error for the 20 m product and a
  re-run of the horizon with heights perturbed by it.
- **Flight Δv and propellant** have no ε of their own. A Monte Carlo over the whole flight would need guided
  correction burns (an open-loop translunar flight disperses by thousands of kilometres), which the app does not
  model; only the descent is dispersed.
- **The input σ of the dispersion are assumptions.** They are round numbers meant to be replaced by sourced
  engine and navigation data (`DISPERSION_SIGMAS` in `flight/dispersion.ts`).

## How the numbers above were computed

- Moon rotation: v = 2π · 1737.4 km / (655.720 h · 3600 s/h) = 10,916.4 km / 2,360,592 s = 4.624 m/s; times
  cos 86.75° = 0.0567 → 0.262 m/s; cos 85° = 0.0872 → 0.403 m/s; cos 89.46° = 0.0094 → 0.0436 m/s.
- Sun elevation rate near the pole: a site at colatitude c sees the Sun's elevation swing by about ±c over one
  synodic month, so its fastest change is 2π · c / 29.53 d; at c = 3.25° that is 0.0288°/h. An angle error of
  0.0001° then shifts an edge by 0.0001 / 0.0288 h = 12.5 s, and 0.001° by 125 s.
- Station side: 0.0763° / (15.04°/h · cos 40.43°) = 24.0 s (Madrid DSS-54), 0.0763° / (15.04°/h · cos 35.40°)
  = 22.4 s (Canberra DSS-34). Station latitudes 40.43° and −35.40° from `app/public/data/stations.json`.
- Sampling error of a standard deviation from N = 200 samples (normal approximation): 1/√(2 · 199) = 0.050.
